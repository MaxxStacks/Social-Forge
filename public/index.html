04 / GOOD TO KNOW

Questions?
We've got you.

Everything in the live preview is clearly labeled. We don't ask for social passwords or pretend supplier orders have been fulfilled.

Visit the help center ↗
Can I buy services right now?

Not yet. The product selector and cart are interactive demonstrations. Actual purchases require vetted suppliers, approved products and a payment provider.

Where will the services come from?

We are building integrations with wholesale service APIs. The live supplier catalog is separate from demonstration products.

Can I choose a custom amount?

Yes, the demo package selector includes presets and custom quantities. Real offers will follow each provider's supported limits.

Do I need to give you my password?

No. Never enter social network account passwords into this storefront. A future eligible service would request only necessary public links or other permitted order parameters.
