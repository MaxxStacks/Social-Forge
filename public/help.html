What exactly can I buy from SocialEngine?

Nothing yet. This is a preview. Once supplier accounts and approved products are connected, eligible services will appear automatically with their scope, pricing and quantity limits. Checkout remains disabled until fulfillment and payments are ready.

Where do the services come from?

SocialEngine is built to ingest actual products from authenticated wholesale service-provider APIs. Imported information includes a supplier service ID, service category, price per 1,000, minimum/maximum quantities and order type.

Why is the product catalog empty?

No supplier API credentials have been connected in the hosting environment. The site does not fabricate products or prices as placeholders.

Can I order without giving my password?

Planned ordering for eligible services will require a public account or post link, not your social media password. Do not share your account passwords with any third-party reseller.

Can I choose my own quantity?

Yes, the product details preview will allow a custom quantity between the limits returned by the supplier. Prices are estimates until currency, fees and supplier reliability have been verified.

How can I track an order?

Order placement and tracking are not yet active. The planned system will attach a unique order ID to each accepted purchase and synchronize delivery states with the provider API.

Will refills, refunds or cancellations be automatic?

These depend on the specific supplier's capabilities and commercial terms. SocialEngine will not promise refunds or refills that a connected provider cannot support. The customer-facing workflows are still under development.

Do you offer cashback or reward points?

Not yet. Coupons, rewards and account balances are planned but won't be represented as active until transaction recording and fraud protections are in place.

Can I use the free tools?

Yes. The social link checker and caption counter work directly in your browser and require no account.

Are you affiliated with the listed platforms?

No. SocialEngine is independently operated. Any provider service that violates a social platform's rules may be excluded from sale.
