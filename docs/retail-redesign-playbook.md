# SocialForge retail redesign playbook — reference review, 17 October 2026

## What Graming does well (public pages reviewed)
Reference pages:
- https://graming.com/
- https://graming.com/buy-instagram-followers/
- https://graming.com/buy-instagram-likes/
- https://graming.com/buy-tiktok-followers/
- https://graming.com/faq/

**Homepage:** recognizable platform navigation, direct service-level links, a focused value proposition, and clearly linked service categories. The customer does not need to understand the provider integration or automation infrastructure.

**Product landing pages:** a descriptive product title, easy-to-scan category selection, preset quantity packages, custom quantity, total-price presentation, a prominent primary action, short service terms, and questions answered below the shopping section. The same pattern repeats across Instagram and TikTok service pages.

**Retention and support:** Graming promotes an account, order tracking, support, coupon/cashback, and loyalty concepts; these are claims/features on its pages, not proof of supplier quality.

## Redesign mandate
- **The product should be obvious within three seconds.** No "command center", "AI engine", "reengineered commerce" or other technical jargon in shopper-facing hero copy.
- Put recognizable network navigation and concrete service names before explanatory marketing material.
- In every product header, show one title, one selection panel, and one unambiguous next step.
- Use restrained typography, generous white space, photographic or crafted commerce imagery only where it supports the product, and consistent controls. Avoid generic neon dashboards, fake metrics and decorative AI-generated abstractions.
- On product pages, arrange choices in the natural order: platform > service > package/quantity > price/requirements > next step.
- Make the package view fully usable on mobile without forcing users to navigate multiple independent tools.
- Keep engineering details, supplier diagnostics, AI, and developer status away from the main sales journey. Place operational readiness in a separate route for the owner.
- Preserve accessible labels, keyboard focus, input validation, and consistent responsive breakpoints.
- Never invent sales numbers, customer reviews, discount percentages, delivery speeds or processor acceptance.
- Provider offerings and prices only become saleable after real data ingestion, review and approval. Existing simulated inventory must remain visibly labeled and must never accept payments.

## Acceptance gates for a stronger design
1. The first mobile viewport identifies the platform/service and has an obvious browse or configure action.
2. One service page works on its own as an entry point from Google, without requiring a homepage visit.
3. Preset and custom quantity controls show a consistent, correctly calculated estimate and constraints, when a supported provider rate exists.
4. No ambiguous active "buy" action if a product cannot be purchased.
5. Desktop and 390px mobile browser screenshots are reviewed, not just unit tests.
6. Primary navigation and the product configuration path are tested on touch, keyboard and desktop.

## Critical distinction
Graming's terms say that its standard followers/likes/views are artificial, not real interactions. Do not reproduce misleading promises about authenticity; do not infer platform permission from a competitor's marketing.
