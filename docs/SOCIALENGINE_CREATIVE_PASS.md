# Expressive SocialEngine redesign

Owner feedback supersedes the earlier request for typography-only polish. The new direction is an expressive, creator-focused retail experience using exactly #275ddf, Syne display type and DM Sans body type. No gradients or copied competitor artwork.

## Delivered in this branch

- Original transparent creator artwork, a blue editorial hero, platform selection and purposeful motion with reduced-motion support.
- Followers, views and engagement merchandising, a server-priced package explorer, illustrative dashboard, platform navigation and richer editorial sections.
- Shared full navigation for both the desktop hamburger and Services control, searchable 16-network menu, native modal focus containment, Escape dismissal and focus restoration.
- Shared four-column footer, oversized brand signature and copyright/disclosure bar.
- Engine guided assistant: public service information and direct links. Deterministic answers, clearly labeled; no AI model, human agent, private account access or third-party data transmission is implied.
- Shared typography and chrome apply to login, service browsing and product pages.

## Commercial state

The owner selected **30% extra on the first deposit** as a future offer. Do not publish or activate it yet. Design quality is the immediate priority. No bonus popup, payment feature or funded balance was added.

Existing PostgreSQL account functionality remains available on the Railway preview. Purchases, deposits and supplier fulfillment remain disabled. No growth outcome, customer count or review is invented. The creator and dashboard visuals are labeled illustrative.
